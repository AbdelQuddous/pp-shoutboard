Privacy Policy

Shout: LED Banner & Big Text (formerly ShoutBoard)

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Shout ("the App") turns your phone into a fullscreen readable sign: scrolling LED banners, big text, QR codes, playlists and exports. The App is free to use with ads, and offers an optional one-time purchase, Shout Pro, that removes all ads.

What stays on your device

Everything you create stays on your device. The App has no accounts and no servers of its own. Stored locally:

  • Your messages, saved signs, playlists and history
  • Themes, colors and display settings
  • Home-screen widget and Quick Settings tile content
  • Ad frequency counters and whether you own Shout Pro

Your messages are never uploaded or shared with advertisers. This data is deleted when you uninstall the App.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads never appear while a sign is being shown. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • App interactions with ads, diagnostics and performance data

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and regions with similar laws are asked for consent through Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Buying Shout Pro turns off all ads and stops ad requests.

Purchases

Shout Pro is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; the App never sees your payment details. The App only receives confirmation that you own Shout Pro.

Permissions

  • Microphone: used only when you tap voice dictation, or turn on beat sync during a show. Dictation uses your device's speech-recognition service, which may process audio according to your device settings. Beat sync reads only the sound level in real time; the App does not record, store or send audio.
  • Media and storage: used only when you export a sign as an image, GIF or video, or pick a file to import.
  • Advertising ID: used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the free version, as described above.

Sharing

The App can receive text or images shared from other apps, and can share signs you create through the Android share sheet. Nothing you create is sent anywhere unless you choose to share it.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google User Messaging Platform: ads and consent
  • Google Play Billing: the Shout Pro purchase

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and is intended for users aged 13 and over.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes are posted on this page with an updated date.
